# Choose Your Own Adventure

A text-based interactive fantasy adventure where players create a character, navigate branching crossroads, purchase equipment and items, and battle a dragon.

## Language

**Healing Potion**:
A consumable magical draught that fully restores the Player to their Maximum Health Points when used in combat.
_Avoid_: Health potion, medicine, elixir, potion bottle

**Maximum Health Points**:
The permanent upper bound of a Player's life force established at character creation, which cannot be exceeded by healing.
_Avoid_: Max HP, health ceiling, max life

**Merchant**:
A woodland vendor situated in the southern glade who sells magical armor, upgraded weapons, and healing potions.
_Avoid_: Shopkeeper, trader, store

**Dragon**:
The fierce adversary encountered on the northern mountain peak.
_Avoid_: Boss, monster, beast

**Ancient Shrine**:
A stone sanctuary situated to the east of the mountain crossroads where players may attempt a trial to recover a Healing Potion.
_Avoid_: Temple, ruins, altar, fountain
