# 03: Occupation-Tailored Magical Weapon Progression

**What to build:**
An upgraded tier of magical weapons tailored to each character occupation (+6 MaxDamage with custom ASCII art). Equipping the upgraded weapon allows players to deal higher damage against the dragon and displays the weapon's localized title and ASCII art across character summaries, combat screens, and attack animations.

**Blocked by:** None (can start immediately)

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Fighter receives flaming broadsword (MaxDamage: 18, ASCII art: `~*~-)=====>`)
- [ ] Magician receives meteor strike spell (MaxDamage: 18, ASCII art: `(((*)))~~~`)
- [ ] Thief receives shadowblade dagger (MaxDamage: 12, ASCII art: `}=---=>`)
- [ ] Archer receives elven composite bow (MaxDamage: 14, ASCII art: `}}==-->`)
- [ ] Player attack rolls and damage calculations against the dragon scale with the upgraded weapon's maximum damage
- [ ] Upgraded weapon types and ASCII art appear in character summary and combat status screens
- [ ] All upgraded weapon names are registered and localized in display maps across English, Spanish, French, and Italian
- [ ] Unit tests verify weapon stats, damage rolls, and display map translations
